01 / A WORLD BEYOND

A doorway with a pulse

Gold light, violet mist and curious spirits greet your Keeper. Check the mysterious portal in the game to see what slips through.

02 / YOUR NEXT MOVE

Battles that fit your screen

Portrait-led combat, character popups and clearer command menus. Phone layouts give allies, enemies and timed actions more breathing room.

03 / FIND YOUR WAY

Explore one doorway at a time

Readable map labels, mist over unexplored places and a quieter first-watch guide help you find your next step.

A player economy to grow into

Fish, craft, buy and sell at the quartermaster, or exchange trade goods with other Keepers at Hall’s Edge. Multiplayer trade supplies have their own satchel.

Face another Keeper

Try saved-team arena challenges, guild tournaments and two-Keeper-per-side guild battles. Tournament PvP currently uses fixed test loadouts, with no entry fee or prizes.

More than the next fight

Collect companions, explore, join a fellowship or guild, and visit Honest Drew’s Tavern for rest and side activities.

Your journey travels with you

Link your account to bring cloud progress between browsers. Existing linked Keepers keep their progress.
